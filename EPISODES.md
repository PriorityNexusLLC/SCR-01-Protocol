## What the series is

Each episode takes a trait that gets pathologised in neurodivergent people —
masking, burnout, hyperfocus, people-pleasing — and re-describes it through the
architecture of a machine: processors, voltage, emulation layers, graph vs.
indexed databases, thermal load. The point is a reframe: these traits aren't
defects in a broken system; they're the behaviour of a *differently optimised*
one.

The episodes are also where much of the SCR framework was worked out in plain
language — the Consent Polygon, the Whimsy Differential, the dual-processor
model of cognition — so the series doubles as the accessible companion to the
technical work in this repository.
