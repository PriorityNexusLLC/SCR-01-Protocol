The canonical equation

The macro and the micro black box

This is the part worth being explicit about, because the documents themselves draw the line:

The macro — the Unified Matrix — is released in full. The governing equation, the stability argument, the derivation of every dimension. Enough to understand the system, check the math, derive from it, and build around it.
The micro — the internal definition of D(X), the Target Model Kinetic Drive (the UCE internal dynamics) — is the black box. It appears in the equation as a term, and the derivation table documents it as the source each dimension traces back to, but its standalone internal definition is deliberately not expanded here.

So: you get the macro that drives everything, and how all the parts fall out of it. The inner engine of the drive term stays closed. That's not evasion — it's the one boundary kept on purpose, stated plainly, which is the only honest way to hold a boundary at all.
